A few quick answers.

Do I need Spotify Premium?+

Only for Spotify audio. Connect Volta for matched lossless audio, or use free YouTube Music. The native demo needs no account or audio device. YouTube playback needs yt-dlp, FFmpeg, and Deno; the music catalog adapter needs Python 3.10+. Tuitify prepares missing playback tools automatically.

Does Spotify Desktop need to be open?+

No. Tuitify streams and plays audio directly, with Windows media key support.

Where does my data go?+

Your preferences, queues, and listening totals stay on your PC. There’s no analytics collection. Discord presence is enabled by default and can share song details with your local Discord client.
