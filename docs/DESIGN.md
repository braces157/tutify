## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an avant-garde editorial studio aesthetic blending disciplined Swiss International typographic principles with raw digital brutalism. Designed for visionary modern tech brands, creative laboratories, and next-generation engineering teams, it communicates hyper-precision, unflinching taste, and intellectual clarity.

Key aesthetic drivers:
- **Swiss Precision meets Digital Brutalism:** Extreme typographic hierarchy, disciplined baseline alignment, stark architectural borders, and generous negative space contrasting tightly metered modular cells.
- **Architectural Structure:** Visible technical rules, hairline grid divisions, numbered sequencing markers, and terminal micro-annotations.
- **Atmospheric Tension:** Predominantly deep monochrome or clinical stark surfaces illuminated by surgical phosphor terminal accents, commanding attention to raw deliverables, code artifacts, and craftsmanship.

## Colors
The palette balances severe monochromatic depth against electric phosphor signals:

- **Primary (`#00FF88` / `phosphor-emerald` `#10B981`):** High-voltage terminal accents used for live statuses, interactive states, execution indicators, and critical focus triggers.
- **Secondary (`#3B5BD9`):** Deep cobalt studio blue delivering intellectual anchor points, pill tags, and secondary action framing.
- **Tertiary & Accents (`#F5C518`, `#E0492A`):** Studio utility badges, experimental markers, and categorical highlights.
- **Neutral & Structural (`#0A0A0A`, `#121212`, `#FFFFFF`):** Deep carbon backgrounds with pure white typographic cut-outs and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` hairline architectural rules.

## Typography
Typography is the structural hero of this design system:

- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Tight, geometric, high-impact sans-serif evoking brutalist European poster design and engineered precision. Major display heads feature tight tracking and compact line-heights.
- **Editorial Body (`Work Sans`):** Clean, neutral, highly legible sans text balancing high readability with understated functional elegance.
- **Technical Annotations (`JetBrains Mono`):** Step markers (e.g., `01`, `02`), metadata headers, pill labels, and terminal outputs are strictly set in uppercase monospaced type with generous tracking.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout operates on a disciplined 12-column modular grid with architectural hairline divides separating thematic acts:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 48px canvas margin, 24px column gutters. Major content splits follow asymmetrical editorial ratios (e.g., 5-column thesis statement paired with 7-column exposition or 4x3 showcase card matrices).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** 32px margins, 20px gutters. Showcase blocks collapse to 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 20px margins, 16px gutters with edge-to-edge perimeter lines. Vertical stacking prioritizes giant display titling over immediate body context.
- **Modular Rhythm:** Consistent top-and-bottom hairline dividers demarcate sections with `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` padding to preserve spatial tension and editorial gravitas.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through strict planar hierarchy and contrast rather than soft ambient shadows:

- **Hairline Grid Architecture:** Surfaces are segregated using crisp `1px` structural borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Surface Elevation:** Base canvas sits at `#0A0A0A`; elevated showcase panels and card matrices shift to `#121212`.
- **Phosphor Accents & Radiance:** Focused cards and active terminal elements apply an ultra-subtle, surgical phosphor glow (`0 0 20px rgba(0, 255, 136, 0.15)`), evoking high-end hardware interfaces and terminal consoles.

## Shapes
The core system embraces strict brutalist geometry:

- **Containers & Modules:** `0px` border-radius for cards, panels, layout modules, and dividers, preserving razor-sharp architectural edges.
- **Micro Tags & Capsule Buttons:** Full circular pill radii (`9999px`) reserved exclusively for category chips, interaction pills, and primary CTAs to create purposeful tension between sharp frames and fluid triggers.

## Components

### Buttons & Interaction CTAs
- **Primary Pill Action:** Jet-black or dark container wrapped in a 1px border with high-contrast text (`#FFFFFF` or `#00FF88`), `border-radius: 9999px`, and `JetBrains Mono` or tight `Space Grotesk` uppercase text. On hover: invert fill to `#00FF88` with `#0A0A0A` text.
- **Brutalist Outline Button:** Sharp 0px corners, hairline white border, monospaced step prefix (`[ENTER] →`).

### Showcase Cards
- Dark planar blocks (`#121212`) bounded by 1px rules with full-bleed aspect ratios (1:1 or 4:5).
- Micro-labels (`JetBrains Mono`, 12px uppercase) positioned above bold headlines, accompanied by modular summary copy and category pill groups at the bottom edge.

### Chips & Monospaced Tags
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) inline tags filled with solid accents (`#3B5BD9`, `#E0492A`, `#F5C518`) or subtle translucent borders, sized at 11px uppercase monospace for quick categorical indexing.

### Stepper & Index Nodes
- Discrete circular index discs with numbered counters (`01`, `02`, `03`) in JetBrains Mono.
- Placed directly above concept headlines with a continuous hairline connecting axis.

### Input Fields & Terminal Controls
- Flat border-bottom or full rectangular hairline inputs with monospaced caret blinks in `#00FF88`. Zero background clutter with stark uppercase placeholder hints.
